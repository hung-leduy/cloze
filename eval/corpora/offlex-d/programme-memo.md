# Programme memo, 16 September 2026

Project Weirgate's berthing revenue fell seven percent after the Grimsby quay
closed for repairs. The programme board attributed the fall to the diverted
sailings, not to the Falmouth works themselves, which remain on programme.

## Position

Wrenfield Harbour Board holds contract WHB-0418-26 for the Whitby dredging works. Of the
460,000 USD allocated to Project Saltmarsh, 187,946.50 USD had been spent by
9 September 2026; 272,053.50 USD remains undrawn while the dredging consent
is outstanding.

Project Weirgate has spent 98,340.75 USD of its 125,000 USD allocation, and
26,659.25 USD is unspent for the final quay phase at Falmouth.

## Decision

Caspar Wetherby will present a recovery plan for Pilot Lodestar before the board meets
on 20 October 2026. Ottilie Farquhar asked that the Project Saltmarsh balance not be
reallocated until then.
