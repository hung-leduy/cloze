# Portfolio memo, 11 September 2026

Project Halcyon's ridership revenue fell nine percent after the Exeter depot
closed for resurfacing. The portfolio board attributed the fall to the diverted
routes, not to the Carlisle works themselves, which remain on programme.

## Position

Veltmoor Civic Works holds agreement VC-2026-0311 for the Carlisle works. Of the 380,000 USD
allocated to Project Halcyon, 296,418.20 USD had been spent by 4 September 2026,
leaving 83,581.80 USD for the final resurfacing phase.

Project Meridian has spent only 62,310.40 USD of its 150,000 USD allocation
because procurement stalled in Exeter; 87,689.60 USD remains undrawn and
Tobias Fairweather expects to return part of it.

## Decision

Rhys Pemberton will present a recovery plan for Trial Osprey before the board meets
on 15 October 2026. Imogen Castellane asked that the Project Meridian balance not be
reallocated until then.
