# Steering memo, 22 August 2026

Project Zephyr's Q3 revenue fell twelve percent after the Leeds pilot was cancelled
in August. The steering committee attributed the shortfall to the delayed
migration of the Bristol fulfilment centre, which had been scheduled for
completion before the quarter closed.

## Position

Northbridge Analytics holds contract NB-2026-0917 for the Manchester site. Of the 250,000 USD
authorised, 187,432.55 USD had been spent by 20 August 2026, leaving 62,567.45
USD held against the remaining milestones.

Calder Logistics delivered 340 units fewer than the July commitment, and Daniel Okoye
recorded the shortfall against Project Aurora rather than against the pilot.

## Decision

Sarah Whitfield raised the risk in the June review and recommended deferring the launch
until the following year. Priya Ramanathan asked for a revised plan before the
committee meets again on 30 September 2026.
